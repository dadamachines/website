    <p class="tbd-section-label">The instrument is your world</p>
    <div class="tbd-intro-grid">
      <h2 id="how-title">From a MIDI note to a real sound.</h2>
      <div class="tbd-prose">
        <p>Connect your DAW, music app or hardware MIDI instrument to automat. Connect a solenoid beater to one of its 12 outputs, place it against something that sounds interesting, and send a note.</p>
        <p>The controller turns MIDI into movement. The object makes the sound. Change the surface, the rhythm or the way you mount the beater, and you have a different instrument.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="automat-steps">
      <article><span>01</span><h3>Connect</h3><p>USB or DIN MIDI from your existing music setup.</p></article>
      <article><span>02</span><h3>Build</h3><p>Connect beaters and choose the objects they play.</p></article>
      <article><span>03</span><h3>Play</h3><p>Sequence notes, experiment with rhythms and record the acoustic result.</p></article>
    </div>

  <section class="automat-use" id="in-use" aria-labelledby="use-title">
    <div class="tbd-wrap">
      <p class="tbd-section-label">Play, compose, perform</p>
      <h2 id="use-title">Your setup. Your kind of music machine.</h2>
      <p class="automat-lead">From a first experiment on a kitchen table to a theatre production or a live installation, automat gives physical sounds a place in your MIDI workflow.</p>
    </div>
    <article class="automat-use-row automat-play">
      <div class="tbd-story automat-edge-copy">
        <h3>Play. It's fun, for everyone.</h3>
        <p>Never played an instrument? Not a problem. Music apps make it easy to start exploring rhythms and sounds with the objects around you.</p>
        <p>In November 2014, Johannes held a workshop with friends in Lund, Sweden. Seeing non-musicians get excited and experiment with sound helped inspire the dadamachines project.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="embed-container"><iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/It_b0GDMACo" title="Playing and experimenting with the automat toolkit" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>
    </article>
    <article class="automat-use-row automat-compose">
      <div class="embed-container"><iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/ER4nbQy69Xk" title="Composing with acoustic objects and the automat toolkit" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>
      <div class="tbd-story automat-edge-copy">
        <h3>Compose. Create your own sound.</h3>
        <p>Ready to get hands-on? Use digital sequencing to try acoustic ideas precisely and quickly. Combine instruments, materials and everyday objects to make a sound that's your own.</p>
        <p>Taison, an old friend of Johannes, composes for theatre productions. His work on “20.000 Meilen unter den Meeren” became an early testing ground for the toolkit.</p>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article class="automat-use-row automat-perform">
      <div class="tbd-story automat-edge-copy">
        <h3>Perform. Build live shows & installations.</h3>
        <p>Create a multi-sensory live experience, or chain controllers to build a larger music machine. The same MIDI workflow can grow from a few beaters to an installation.</p>
        <p>In early 2016, Johannes was approached by BLACKSPACE to develop the MUSIC BOX installation for Audi. It brought the toolkit into a live performance setting.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="embed-container"><iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/bstnULC-JVg" title="Automat-powered MUSIC BOX installation with BLACKSPACE for Audi" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>
